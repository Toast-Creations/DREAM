# Who Makes the Unusual Idea Useful?

*DREAM3 · E3 · Generator × developer capacity*

> **Status: proposal, not a result.** This study has not been run. It is published as written on 1 November 2026 so the design can be read and criticised before any data exist. See [the proposals index](README.md).

David Cook · 1 November 2026 · Research proposal v1.1.0

## Abstract

We propose a paired factorial study separating the model that generates an idea from the model that develops it. Small and large generators produce ordinary, higher-temperature, and targeted-steering ledgers. Every unchanged ledger is independently developed by small and large unsteered checkpoints. The experiment tests whether additional development capacity recovers value from unusual starting material, rather than conflating better seed generation with better refinement. Source survival and unrelated replacement are separate outcomes.

## Rationale and current evidence

Iterative refinement can improve outputs \[1\], but it can also change the central proposition. DREAM's historical pairwise reassessment favors ordinary and temperature controls on developable material; the newer pilot finals remain unassessed. The completed review-versus-develop comparison has zero qualifying yield under a compromised legacy instrument and retains develop only by default. Neither result establishes which model size or workflow best develops unusual seeds. The hypothesis is a positive developer-size interaction in steering's advantage; equal gains across conditions and productive replacement are competing explanations.

## Design

Cross two generator sizes, three generation arms, and two developer sizes. The planning allocation is 24 tasks × three generation seeds × two generators × three arms: 432 unique ledgers, each forked into two branches, producing 864 developed chains. Preserve verbatim candidates, task instructions, source IDs, and the complete ledger in both development calls. Use fresh contexts, a frozen paired development-seed schedule, identical prompts, and equal token allowances. Developers receive no generation-method labels or sibling branch outputs.

Reuse compatible E2 ledgers only with explicit shared-data identifiers. A fork is another developed observation of the same seeds, not an independent generation attempt. Use compatible same-family small/large checkpoints where possible and report remaining training differences. Reserve the future E5 held-out checkpoint from both roles. Freeze assessment and parsing only after separate content, scope, lineage, and duplication qualification, informed by E4. Do not resume a failed historical instrument.

## Outcomes and analysis

Apply E1's candidate AI-rated useful-survival endpoint, with its qualification and validation limits. Record faithful development, productive reinterpretation, unrelated replacement, duplication, and loss. Assess final content before revealing the linked initial ledger for lineage. Source names alone do not prove continuity. Report total qualifying final usefulness separately: a developer can improve the final answer by inventing something unrelated without recovering the original seed.

For each generator size, compute the C−A gain under the large developer minus that gain under the small developer. Average equally across generator sizes for the primary interaction. C−B is a prespecified corroborating interaction with multiplicity controlled if used for a joint efficacy claim. Average generation seeds within task, retain fork pairing during task-level resampling, and report all cells. Generator-size effects and the three-way interaction are secondary; do not select favorable subgroups after inspection.

Predeclare a worthwhile interaction and finalize task count from development-only precision estimates. The current zero-floor ratings cannot supply reliable variance for a valid usefulness endpoint. Missing assessments remain missing; model-generated losses remain outcomes. Include length, tokens, latency, and resource use by stage. Equal token allowances do not justify calling a small-generator/large-developer configuration cheaper without measuring it.

## Interpretation and contribution

A positive interaction supports extra developer capacity as a way to recover more judged value from steered seeds under the tested protocol. Uniform developer gains favor general refinement capacity. Increased total usefulness with no increased survival identifies replacement rather than recovery. A generator interaction without a developer interaction instead locates the difference in seed production. These are workflow findings; they do not demonstrate a distinct internal creativity mechanism or persistent learning. Publish all paired branches and the original ledger so that these explanations remain auditable.
