# Does Steering Improve with Scale?

*DREAM3 · E2 · Scaling the added benefit*

> **Status: proposal, not a result.** This study has not been run. It is published as written on 1 November 2026 so the design can be read and criticised before any data exist. See [the proposals index](README.md).

David Cook · 1 November 2026 · Research proposal v1.1.0

## Abstract

We propose a within-family comparison of how generator size changes the added benefit of activation steering after a common development process. Three checkpoints will generate ideas under strong prompting, higher-temperature sampling, targeted steering, and random perturbations. A single fixed developer will refine every ledger. The estimand is the change in steering's advantage over credible controls, rather than an increase in absolute model quality. Current Qwen3-8B and Mistral-7B pilots do not constitute a size ladder and provide no scaling estimate.

## Rationale and hypothesis

A larger generator could supply richer unusual relationships, need less steering, or be disrupted more by an intervention. Refusal-steering results show that increasing size need not improve steering efficacy \[1\]; creativity results also depend on task and intervention \[2\]. The directional hypothesis is that the added useful-survival benefit grows with size. Flat, declining, irregular, and ceiling-limited patterns are explicit alternatives. Neither a 7B/8B cross-family comparison nor fluent examples discriminate these possibilities.

## Design

Select three dense checkpoints from one family with comparable training lineage and reasoning settings. Freeze immutable weights/tokenizer revisions, precision, hook sites, and a common developer checkpoint. Extract model-specific directions; numeric coefficients need not imply comparable behavioral dose. Allocate equal development-only setting searches to targeted steering and temperature sampling at each size. Random controls use the selected targeted schedule and several frozen directions. Publish setting-selection criteria and all search results.

The planning matrix is 24 shared tasks × three seeds × four arms × three generator sizes: 864 chains. All cells request five candidates and use the same two unsteered development calls, source ledger, and token allowances. Match task/seed blocks across checkpoints. E1-compatible cells may be reused with their identities disclosed; this is shared evidence, not another independent replication. E4 validation and a separately qualified content/lineage instrument precede efficacy analysis. Preserve model failures as outcomes.

## Outcomes and analysis

Use E1's explicitly labeled AI-rated surviving-idea endpoint and independent validation evidence. At size s, estimate ΔA(s)=mean(C−A) and ΔB(s)=mean(C−B). The co-primary interactions are ΔA(large)−ΔA(small) and ΔB(large)−ΔB(small). Treat size as categorical in the primary analysis. A fitted trend against log parameter count is a descriptive secondary analysis, not a universal scaling law.

Average seeds within tasks and preserve the complete paired task block when computing uncertainty. Use simultaneous intervals for the two interactions, selected before confirmation. Report all three checkpoints, both principal controls, random-direction variability, completion, ceiling effects, realized intervention exposure, and judge-family sensitivity. Choose the final number of independent tasks from development-only precision simulations. Equal token allowances match output opportunity but not FLOPs, latency, or cost; report resources separately.

## Decision and interpretation

A growing advantage requires positive size interactions and a credible useful-survival gain over both controls at the larger checkpoint. Higher final quality in every arm establishes an absolute size association, not a steering interaction. If the fixed developer limits every generator, interpret the result conditional on that developer and examine E3 rather than silently changing development capacity mid-study. A negative small-model result does not rule out a larger-model gain, but it weakens claims made without a direct test.

Checkpoint training differences remain confounded with parameter count even within a family. A second-family replication is separate work. Three observed sizes support only a limited empirical trend; E5 tests a separately withheld larger checkpoint. Do not use that future checkpoint for developer selection, direction construction, calibration outcomes, or inspection before the forecast's information boundary is fixed. Publish null and negative interactions as well as positive ones.
