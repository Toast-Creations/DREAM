# From Strange to Useful

*DREAM3 · E1 · Controlled idea survival*

> **Status: proposal, not a result.** This study has not been run. It is published as written on 1 November 2026 so the design can be read and criticised before any data exist. See [the proposals index](README.md).

David Cook · 1 November 2026 · Research proposal v1.1.0

## Abstract

Does temporary activation steering provide better starting material for useful development? We propose a fresh controlled comparison of strong creative prompting, higher-temperature sampling, targeted steering, and random-direction perturbation. Every arm receives identical unsteered development and finalization. The primary endpoint is distinct, traceable final ideas meeting a qualified assessment rule. The completed two-model pilots establish execution and expose measurement failures; they do not establish effectiveness. Confirmation will begin only after extraction, content assessment, and lineage assessment pass separate qualification.

## Evidence and contribution

Creativity steering and iterative refinement already exist \[1,2\]. DREAM's proposed contribution is useful idea survival under matched development. Current manuscript v0.3 reports 144 completed chains and 432 normally ended stage calls, repaired extraction, and failed scoped-v4 qualification in both judge families. Historical pairwise judgments favor simpler controls on developable material. These findings motivate a fair test; they do not supply a positive prior result or a definitive null for the newer intervention.

## Design

Use one frozen generator and one common unsteered developer, with four ideation arms: A, strong shared prompting at ordinary sampling; B, the same prompt at higher temperature; C, ordinary sampling with targeted steering; D, ordinary sampling with nine balanced random directions. C and D share the recorded site, relative-magnitude schedule, and sampling. Select settings on development-only tasks with equal tuning opportunities for C and B; publish every candidate setting. Treat a comparison with only temperature 1.3 as specific to that baseline, rather than competitively optimized temperature in general.

The planning allocation is 24 new tasks, eight each in constrained design, relational analogy, and artistic concepts, with three seeds and four arms: 288 three-call chains. Retain five initial slots and the complete verbatim ledger in both development calls. Freeze exact prompts, revisions, token allowances, failure handling, task identities, extractor tests, and realized-dose logging before opening confirmation outputs. The allocation requires task-level precision analysis; it is not a power result.

## Measurement and analysis

The candidate primary endpoint is AI-rated qualifying surviving ideas, 0–5 per attempted chain. Both independently qualified model-judge families must score novelty, task value, and coherence at least 3/4, pass applicable individual constraints, and verify substantive source lineage. The joint gate remains a candidate instrument until qualification; scoped-v4 failed and cannot supply it. Novelty must not be substituted for value. Set size and diversity are assessed separately from individual members. Qualify lineage on unseen mergers, reinterpretations, replacements, duplicates, and misleading source labels.

Use one designated primary assessment per idea per judge. A deterministic 10% repeat audit in development diagnoses stability without changing the designated primary score; freeze the eventual confirmation audit policy separately. E4 supplies external validation, not permission to relabel inspected outputs. Unrelated replacements receive separate final-usefulness credit and no survival credit. Preserve missing generated members, unassessed members, and failed provider calls as different states; report bounds where assessments are missing.

Average seeds within task. Estimate paired C−A and C−B effects with task-level uncertainty and a frozen multiplicity adjustment. An added-value claim requires both comparisons; C−D is a direction-specificity check. Report the full score distributions, single-family sensitivities, completion, delivery, duplicates, unrelated replacement, tokens, and runtime. The provisional worthwhile gain is 0.5 qualifying ideas per five requested slots; assess precision relative to that value.

## Decision and limits

If qualification fails, stop endpoint collection and report the measurement failure. A positive effect on this AI-rated endpoint does not establish human utility, historical originality, an optimal schedule, persistent learning, or benefits at other sizes. Publish all arms and attempts. Existing inspected pilots remain development evidence; a local freeze is not a public preregistration. Register the final design before fresh confirmation. This proposal supersedes the earlier three-human-rater description for this prospective AI-endpoint design without amending any historical run.
