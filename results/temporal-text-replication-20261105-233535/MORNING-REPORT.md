Overall, 4,386 / 4,686 (93.6%) were exact; 4,624 were accepted, 44 unmapped and 18 rejected. The dominant misread writes the arrival as a verified *state*. As in the short study, this creates a verified–verified conflict, and the truthful reading then gives empty support and a decline. Across the panel: 195 empty-support copies (changing), 296 copies with a historical conflict, and 116 with pending evidence.

**Harms (reported beside utility, as required).** Text produced **3 invalid actual attempts and 1–2 false completions per cell** (stable cells: 3 invalid, 2 false; changing cells: 3 invalid, 1 false). Structured produced none. They come from **5 distinct goal copies**, which repeat across environments and selectors:
- **Invalid `install` (3 copies: b001 a06/A, b015 a06/A, b021 a04/B).** "Part X was taken by another team" was extracted as a verified *correction emptying a slot* (or another part's stock). The planner then installed into a slot that was not empty.
- **False completion (b018 a08/A).** "Part X was delivered to you" was extracted as an *event installing X into the goal slot*, so `already` was chosen.
- **False completion (b021 d05/A, stable).** A crate move was attributed to *the wrong crate*.

In all five, the parsed support was fully represented, confidently wrong, and excluded the actual state. P2 never queries such a field, and DEPENDENCY does not either: it is not uncertain under the parsed history. This is the "wrong settled-looking extraction" blind spot the brief named. The harms are identical under BASE and DEPENDENCY; selection neither causes nor prevents them.
